What it is

Delper Integrated is the parent programme in the Delper Group Executive Summary. It brings four initiatives into a single economic engine. The proposal targets logistics friction, a global estimated at USD 800 billion a year in administrative drag, and industrial cyber exposure on legacy operational technology.

Who owns it

A consolidated holding company, Top-Co, would hold 100% downstream control. Radiant Synage would hold a 60% majority and contribute USD 12 million in IP, banking infrastructure and core lines. The investor would take 40%, as a significant minority.

What it builds

  • Project Iron Nexus, USD 40T A multimodal transport grid linking deepwater ports, electrified rail corridors, express highways and air cargo hubs. Rail freight is designed to cut transport energy use by 60%.
  • Project Make in India, USD 56T A coastal network of about 560,000 automated factories on one-acre sites, powered by 840 GW of dedicated nuclear baseload, along a 7,500 km corridor. The proposal projects a 28% share of world physical manufacturing value-add.
  • Delper Aegis An enterprise industrial cybersecurity layer. Each production line sits behind an inline firewall targeting IEC 62443 Security Level 4, with a physical write-lock against remote programming. Projected base ARR is USD 247.7 billion at full scale.
  • Delper Global Trust Infrastructure A buyer-first sourcing platform with two tracks. Track A (bulk) earns a 15.184% fee on gross merchandise value, settled on a 14-day cycle. Track B (retail) targets 24.2% gross profit per order, with escrow released after delivery and a satisfaction certificate.

How it is funded and repaid

All of the USD 96 trillion is to be raised as bonds at 9%: USD 40 trillion for Iron Nexus and USD 56 trillion for Make in India. Both series are repaid from a shared net profit pool. Iron Nexus is targeted for retirement around Year 18 and Make in India around Year 20.

The immediate ask

The USD 8 million is split evenly between two tracks. Track A (global consultants setup) covers SAP process and ERP systems, SGS quality-control links, and a USD 2.6 million revolving escrow margin. Track B (India retail pilot) covers engineering, third-party quality control, legal and licensing, supplier vetting, cloud connectors, and a contingency. Track B is released in three tranches of USD 1.0M, 1.5M and 1.5M.

Points to test before relying on it

  • The headline figures are very large. USD 96 trillion is comparable to the entire world economy, and the manufacturing share is a projection.
  • Several partners and approvals are not yet in place: ONDC, SGS, the Axis Bank escrow, and regulatory clearance for the token.
  • The USD 8 million covers only the first phase. Later phases depend on the bond market and the profit pool.
  • The source has internal inconsistencies, including the FDE headcount.
